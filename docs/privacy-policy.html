Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the Tarteel Quran app (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it.

In short

  • The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never sell your data.
  • Reading, listening and memorization (Hifz) work fully on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers unless you turn on the optional teacher or student feature.
  • If you turn it on, your data is stored in Google Firebase and shown only to the teachers you link with yourself.
  • You can delete your cloud data at any time from inside the app, or by emailing us.

1. Data kept only on your device

The following is stored locally on your phone:

  • App settings (language, theme, font sizes, selected reciter, Tajweed colors on/off), your daily ward goal and reminder time.
  • Downloaded recitation audio files and cached Quran text, tafsir and translations.
  • Your daily reading/listening time and your Hifz history (surah, ayah range, repetitions, rating and personal notes).

Settings and downloads never leave your phone. Daily time and Hifz history also stay on your phone unless you turn on student mode, which copies them to your cloud account as described below (including daily totals from before you turned it on).

This data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone settings.

2. Optional teacher & student feature

This feature is off by default. Turning it on creates an anonymous account with Google Firebase Authentication — we do not ask for an email address or password. The account is identified only by a random ID.

3. Who can see your data

  • Teachers you linked with can see your name, phone and WhatsApp numbers, streak, total sessions, daily activity and live activity.
  • Session details are visible only to the teacher you choose when saving a session. Sessions saved as “Private (don’t share)” are stored in your cloud account but are not visible to any teacher.
  • Notes can be read only by the student who receives them.
  • Anyone who knows a teacher’s share code can see that teacher’s display name.
  • We do not share your data with anyone else, except where required by law.

You can unlink a teacher at any time; they immediately lose access to your data.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores teacher/student data. See the Firebase privacy information.
  • Quran content providers. To load surah lists, recitations, tafsir, translations and fonts, the app contacts public services such as api.alquran.cloud, everyayah.com, quranicaudio.com, audio.qurancdn.com, mp3quran.net, GitHub and GitLab. Like any web request, these services receive your device’s IP address; no account data is sent to them.

5. App permissions

  • Internet — download recitations and content, and sync the optional teacher/student feature.
  • Notifications — daily ward reminders and notes from your teacher.
  • Alarms & reminders — deliver your daily reminder at the time you set.
  • Foreground media playback & wake lock — keep recitation playing when the screen is off.

6. Retention & security

Cloud data is kept until you delete it or deactivate the feature. All traffic to Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and database access rules restrict each record to its owner and the teachers they linked with.

7. Children

The app’s Quran reading, listening and memorization features collect no personal data and can be used by anyone. The online teacher/student feature is not intended for children under 13 without the involvement of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided personal information without that consent, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices

  • Use the app without the teacher/student feature — nothing is uploaded.
  • Edit your student details at any time from the student screen.
  • Share each Hifz session with a specific teacher, or keep it private.
  • Unlink teachers, or delete all your cloud data (see below).
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or its deletion by email.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update the effective date above when this policy changes, and highlight significant changes in the app.

Delete your account and data

For users of Tarteel Quran by Mohamed Hassan. Deleting your account permanently removes the data listed below. It cannot be undone.

Delete from inside the app

Students:

  1. Open the app, go to Teacher & Student, then I am a student.
  2. Tap Delete My Cloud Data and confirm.

Teachers:

  1. Open the app, go to Teacher & Student, then I am a teacher / sheikh.
  2. Open the ⋮ menu, tap Deactivate Teacher Mode and confirm.

Delete without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from any address with the subject “Delete my Tarteel Quran data”. Include your name as entered in the app and, for teachers, your share code (for example TQ-7F3K9), so we can find your account. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

michaelhaleem688@gmail.com

What is deleted

  • Student profile (name, phone and WhatsApp numbers), linked teachers, all Hifz sessions, daily activity, live activity and received notes.
  • Teacher profile, display name and share code.
  • The anonymous account itself, once no role remains on the device.

What is kept: nothing is kept on our side. Notes a teacher has already sent to students remain in those students’ accounts until they delete them. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
